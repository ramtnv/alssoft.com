FLAGSHIP SOLUTION

Insta CrDr — Cheque Truncation & Clearing Automation

Transforming conventional banking with real-time, image-based cheque truncation and automated clearing workflows.

CTS-Compliant Banking Solution

Same-Day Cheque Clearance Without Physical Movement

Despite electronic fund transfers, cheques remain primary security instruments worldwide. Conventional clearing takes 5 to 15 days for outstation cheques due to physical transit and manual handling. Insta CrDr truncates physical cheques at the branch, converting them into secure cryptographic images for real-time clearing.

Same-Day Local Clearance
< 24h Outstation Turnaround
Zero Transit Courier Losses
Insta CrDr Core Strengths
  • ✓ Same-day credit minimizes customer waiting
  • ✓ Secure encrypted transmission over bank networks
  • ✓ Automated identification of rogue accounts
  • ✓ Long-term image archival and instant retrieval
  • ✓ Reduced clearing cost per instrument

How the Insta CrDr Architecture Works

01
Scan & Capture

Cheque scanned and stored at branch level.

02
Authentication

Images grouped, verified, and signed.

03
Outward Flow

Encrypted transmission to Clearing House.

04
Consolidation

Bank-wise and branch-wise sorting at hub.

05
Inward Clearance

Electronic transfer to drawee branch for posting.

06
Return Routing

Dishonoured cheques regrouped without paper.
